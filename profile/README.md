## 🧪 About VerbaNexAI Lab

**VerbaNexAI** is a research laboratory at the [Universidad Tecnológica de Bolívar (UTB)](https://www.utb.edu.co), Cartagena, Colombia. We explore the convergence of **Natural Language Processing**, **Artificial Intelligence**, and **computational linguistics** to solve real-world problems in multilingual and low-resource settings.

> *"Verba"* (words) + *"Nex"* (connection) + *"AI"* — connecting language and intelligence.

---

## 🔬 Research Lines

| Line | Description |
|------|-------------|
| 🗣️ **NLP & Computational Linguistics** | Phonesthemes, affect analysis, hate speech, fake news detection |
| 🏆 **Shared Tasks & Competitions** | IberLEF, SemEval, CLEF — multilingual NLP benchmarks |
| 👁️ **Computer Vision** | YOLO models, bacteria classification, agricultural imaging |
| 📊 **Applied AI** | Tourism analytics, energy, smart links, STEM education |
